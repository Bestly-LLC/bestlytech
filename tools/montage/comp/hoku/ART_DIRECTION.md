# HOKU short: art direction

Quiet editorial cards. One idea per scene, read aloud by a calm voice.

- Palette and type come from the HOKU card kit (Newsreader serif for the line, Inter for kicker and CTA), so videos sit beside the Instagram cards.
- Motion is slow and soft: elements rise 36px on a heavily damped spring, the block drifts up 16px across a scene, and everything fades out over the last third of a second.
- No stock footage, no flashy transitions, no captions over the text (the on-screen line is the caption).
- Closing card is fixed in code: "A face mist in a sealed can." with the can rising in and "Join the waitlist. Link in bio."
- Sound: narration on top of a very low ambient pad.
- B-roll (when ordered): a vertical LTX clip plays behind each card, slowly zooming 6%, dissolving in and out through the brand color under a brand-colored veil (55-85%), so the footage is texture and the line always reads. Never the can or any product in the footage.
