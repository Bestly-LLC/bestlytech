# Brand short (InventoryProof, Cookie Yeti, Bestly Cloud): art direction

Same quiet, one-idea-per-card film as HOKU, driven by a `brand` prop so each product looks like its own Instagram cards.

- Palettes, type, wordmark or logo and the closing-card art come from the brand's card kit (the same files the daily post renderers use). Nothing is drawn or invented: logos and mascots are the real files, wordmarks are the brand's own type.
- Motion is slow and soft: elements rise 36px on a heavily damped spring, the block drifts up 16px across a scene, everything fades out over the last third of a second.
- The on-screen line is the caption. No stock footage, no flashy transitions, no extra text.
- Closing card is fixed in code: product name, one plain line about it, the mascot or app icon rising in, the handle and the call to action the brand's posts already use.
- Sound: narration on a very low ambient pad.
- B-roll (when ordered): a vertical clip plays behind each card under a veil of the card color (55-85%), so footage is texture and the line always reads. No faces to camera, no text, signs, logos, brands, no invented app screens or UI; phones and laptops only as dark silhouettes.
- Numbers keep their units on one line (no-break spaces are put in the script).
