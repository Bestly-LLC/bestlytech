# Bestly Studio: what we may say, what we never say

Written 2026-10-06 (Track S of `docs/voice-and-studio-promo-opusplan.md`). Applies to every Bestly Studio promo on Bestly's own accounts
(client `bestly-studio` in Studio), the page `bestly.tech/studio`, and any video, card or caption Spark or a Pi writer makes about it.
Machine copy of the "never" list: `claim_rules` for client `bestly-studio` (all start unapproved; Jared reviews them in Studio >
Settings > Claim rules). Migration: `supabase/migrations/20261006240000_bestly_studio_promo_client.sql`.

## What Studio is (one line, for someone who has never heard of it)
We make your social media posts and short videos. You approve them from your phone. We post them.

## True today (checked against the live Studio on 2026-10-06)
Say these, in plain words. Each line says where it is true.
- **We make the posts.** Image posts, carousels (swipe posts) and short vertical videos, made for the business. (Studio queue, Spark, Montage.)
- **A person at Bestly checks every post before you see it.** Nothing reaches a client board until a Bestly person sends it
  (Drafts > To review > Send). Always say a person checks it; never say it is all automatic.
- **You review on your phone.** Each client gets a private link to their own board. It opens on a phone, can sign in with Face ID,
  and shows what is waiting. (Client board: Review, Record, The next 30 days, Send us an idea.)
- **You see the post the way it will look.** Previews in an Instagram frame, a TikTok frame, or full frame.
- **Two buttons: Approve, or Changes.** Changes takes a note in your own words ("Say it however you'd say it out loud"). The client
  board has no Kill button; killing a post is something Bestly does.
- **Earlier versions are kept.** The board has Versions and Compare; you can go back.
- **A calendar of what is coming.** "The next 30 days".
- **Send us an idea or a clip.** The board has "Send us an idea" and "Record" (things for you to film). Email works too: photos and
  notes sent to studio@bestly.tech arrive in the Studio Inbox, where Bestly picks them up.
- **Posting.** After you approve, a post can go out to your Instagram account (you connect the account once). Auto-posting is a
  switch per client, off until the client and Bestly turn it on; otherwise a Bestly person presses Post. Say only "we post it to your
  Instagram". TikTok is not a posting promise (inbox mode, sandbox-limited today). No promise for Facebook, LinkedIn or anything else.
- **Who it is for.** Small businesses, solo professionals, real estate agents (as an audience; see the real estate rule below).
- **How we work with AI, if asked.** We use software, including AI tools, to draft, design and edit. A person at Bestly checks every
  post. (Fine in the FAQ and when a viewer asks. Do not use "AI" as the headline.)
- **Book a call.** The call is free: https://cloud.bestly.tech/apps/calendar/appointment/BtktQYtGFocY

## Never say (and never show)
1. **No prices.** No dollar figure, "per month", "cheap", "affordable", "free trial", "starting at". Jared has not set public pricing.
   (His private stance of about $50 to $100 a month for one realtor is NOT public.)
2. **No results, numbers or growth promises.** No followers, likes, leads, clients, sales, reach, engagement, "go viral", percentages,
   "x times", "guaranteed". No "you will get / grow / double".
3. **No "AI does everything".** No "fully automated", "autopilot", "hands-free", "set and forget", "AI-powered", "AI-generated",
   "no human involved". A person reviews every post and says so wherever the process comes up.
4. **No client names, quotes, logos, testimonials, ratings or reviews.** Not Centering YOU, not Elizabeth, not any listing client.
   No "trusted by", "loved by", "our clients say". No testimonial without written consent from the person.
5. **No mocked or invented app screens.** Only real screenshots of the real Studio, taken on the `bestly-test` demo client with sample
   posts. Never a real client's board (Centering YOU, listings, re-demo, and so on). A sample post is labeled as a sample.
6. **Real estate.** Agents are a target audience; say "for real estate agents" and stop. Do not imply AI-generated property footage and
   do not promise listing videos, property tours or listing reels: Bestly's real-estate offer is photo-based and is not built yet.
7. **No speed, volume or availability promises.** No "instantly", "in 24 hours", "same day", "24/7", "unlimited", "X posts a week/day".
8. **No platform over-claims.** Instagram posting works today. Do not say "all platforms" or promise Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube,
   Pinterest or X. TikTok and Instagram can be named as formats the videos are made for, not as places we post to.
9. **No privacy or security claims about Studio.** Photos and notes pass through the tools that draft and edit them, so never say
   "private", "never shared", "never stored", "encrypted", "on-device", "we don't train on your data", or similar.
10. **No credentials or team claims.** No "team of experts", "award-winning", "years of experience", "dedicated manager",
    "your own designer". Bestly is a small studio; say nothing bigger.
11. **No compliance claims.** No "compliant", "certified", "Fair Housing compliant", HIPAA, GDPR, and so on.
12. **No "posts without your OK".** Never imply anything goes out before the client approves it.
13. **No no-contract, no-commitment, free-month or cancel-anytime promises.** Terms are not set.
14. **No fake urgency or fear.** No "your competitors are ahead", "you are losing clients", "beat the algorithm".

## Style (every Studio promo)
- Bestly voice: plain-spoken, privacy-first, anti-big-tech, calm. Written for someone who has never heard of Studio: the first
  line must make clear what it does.
- No emoji. No hashtags on the page or in cards (captions follow the account's own rules). A number never splits from its unit
  (use a no-break space, e.g. 9&nbsp;AM, 3&nbsp;posts).
- Name Bestly Studio only on the last card or last paragraph, like every other Bestly product post. Call to action: bestly.tech/studio.
- Nothing auto-posts. Studio promo pieces go to Studio > Drafts > To review (client `bestly-studio`, internal) for Jared.
- When a claim is in doubt, leave it out.
