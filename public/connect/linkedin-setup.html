Bestly Studio · setup

Connect the LinkedIn app

linkedin.com/developers → your app Bestly Studio → Auth tab. Copy the Client ID and the Primary Client Secret into the boxes below. They go straight into the vault; nobody sees them again.

On the same app, the Products tab needs Share on LinkedIn and Sign In with LinkedIn using OpenID Connect (no review needed). Posts go to the profile of whoever connects. Under Auth → OAuth 2.0 settings add this Authorized redirect URL:
https://www.bestly.tech/connect/linkedin.html

Leave the permissions box empty unless LinkedIn says one of them isn't allowed for your app. Separate them with spaces.
