Connect the Pinterest app

developers.pinterest.com → My apps → your app → copy the App ID and the App secret key into the boxes below. They go straight into the vault; nobody sees them again.

On the same app, add this Redirect URI:
https://www.bestly.tech/connect/pinterest.html
New apps start on Trial access, where pins are visible only to you. Apply for Standard access (Pinterest asks for a short demo video of the sign-in and a pin being made) before public posting is switched on.
