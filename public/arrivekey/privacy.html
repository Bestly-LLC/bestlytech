Arrivekey

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 27, 2026

Arrivekey is made by Bestly LLC in Los Angeles. It helps car-sharing hosts hand a car over to a guest: the host sets up the car and the parking spot, and the guest opens a link that shows them where to go, how to get in, and how to start the car.

The short version. We collect what is needed to hand a car to a guest and nothing else. We do not sell your information, we do not share it with advertisers, and we do not track you across other apps or websites. Guests do not need an account and are not asked to create one.

Who this covers

There are two kinds of people in Arrivekey, and we treat their information differently.

  • Hosts — people who own the car and use the Arrivekey app to set it up.
  • Guests — people who open a trip link (or its App Clip) to pick the car up. Guests have no account.

What we collect from hosts

Your account

You sign in with Apple. Apple gives us a unique identifier for you and, if you allow it, your name and an email address. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email," we only ever see the relay address. You can also add a passkey (Face ID or Touch ID) so you do not have to sign in again; a passkey stores a public key on our side, never your face, fingerprint, or a password.

Your cars, spots, and trips

What you type in: car make, model, year, color, and plate; the parking garage or lot, its address, and access instructions; door or gate codes you enter or scan; and the trips you create, including guest first names and pickup and return times. If you scan a garage code with the camera, the photo is read on your phone and only the code itself is saved.

Your car, if you connect it

If you connect a Tesla account, you authorize us through Tesla's own sign-in page. We never see your Tesla password. We keep a token that lets us talk to your car, stored encrypted. With it we can read and show things like battery level, range, whether the doors are locked, software version, and — while a trip is running — the car's location, so a guest can find it. We can also send the commands you or your guest press, such as unlock, precondition the climate, honk, or flash the lights. You can disconnect Arrivekey at any time from your Tesla account, and the connection stops working immediately.

Location on your phone

If you set a pickup spot on the map, the app asks for your location while you are using it. It is used to place that pin, and it is not tracked in the background.

What we collect from guests

A guest opens a link their host sent them. To show that page we need very little:

  • The trip the link points to, and what the host put in it.
  • A record that the page was opened, and which steps were tapped, so the host can see the handoff went through and so we can fix things that break.
  • If the guest presses a car button, the fact that the command was sent and whether the car accepted it.
  • Questions a guest types for their host.

The App Clip version may ask the guest to confirm their location once. That confirmation happens on the guest's phone, it is how iOS decides the Clip is being used at the right place, and the result is not stored by us as a location history.

Guests are not asked for a name, an email address, a phone number, or a payment method to open a trip page.

What we never do

  • We do not sell or rent personal information to anyone.
  • We do not use it for advertising, and there are no third-party ad or analytics trackers in the app.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not build profiles about guests.

Who else sees it

Only the companies that make the product work, and only for that purpose:

  • Supabase — where the database, the login service, and our server code run.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, passkeys, App Clips, and push notifications.
  • Tesla — only if you connect a Tesla, and only for your own car.
  • Vercel — serves the web pages guests open.

We will also hand over information if the law requires it, and we will tell you when we are allowed to.

How long we keep it

  • Trip pages and their guest links stop working shortly after the trip's return time.
  • Door and gate codes are replaced as you update them; old ones are removed.
  • Records of what happened during a trip are kept for 12 months so hosts can look back at a handoff, then deleted.
  • Your account and cars stay until you delete them.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Delete my account. That removes your profile, your cars, your lots, your codes, and your trips, and it revokes any car connection. You can also email us and we will do it for you. Deletion is permanent and finishes within 30 days.

Security

Everything travels over encrypted connections. Car tokens and door codes are stored in an encrypted vault, separate from the rest of the database. Each host can only reach their own rows, enforced by the database itself rather than by app code.

Children

Arrivekey is for adults renting and driving cars. It is not directed at children under 13 years old, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. If you are in California, that includes the rights under the CCPA — and since we do not sell or share personal information, there is nothing to opt out of. Email us and we will take care of it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top and tell hosts in the app before it takes effect.
