Delete your data from Bestly Studio

If you connected a Facebook Page to Bestly Studio (the app "Bestly Cloud Social Engine") and want your data removed, use either option below.

Option 1: Remove the app in Facebook

  1. Open Facebook, go to Settings, then Business Integrations.
  2. Find "Bestly Cloud Social Engine" and click Remove.
  3. Bestly can no longer post to your Page. Then email us (Option 2) if you also want the stored data deleted.

Option 2: Email us

Email privacy@bestly.tech with the subject "Delete my Facebook data" and the name of your Page. We delete the stored Page access token, Page name and Page ID within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What we store

Only the Page name, Page ID, a Page access token and the posts you approved. We never store your Facebook password or read your personal profile.

Connected a YouTube channel?

Open your Google Account permissions and remove Bestly Studio. Bestly can no longer post to your channel. To also have the stored data deleted, email privacy@bestly.tech with the subject "Delete my YouTube data" and your channel name. We delete the stored refresh token, channel ID and channel title within 30 days and reply to confirm. We never store your Google password.
